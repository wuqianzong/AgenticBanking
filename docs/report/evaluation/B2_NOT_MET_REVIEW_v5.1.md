### 44. `cash_withdrawal_charge_1`
**Q:** Why am I charged a fee for getting cash?

**Answer key:**
1. Horizon ATMs charge no fee — A ✓ · B ✓
2. Withdrawing cash on the credit card is a cash advance: 8% fee (amounts under S$5 exempt) plus daily interest from posting — A ✗ · B ✗

**Reply:**
> There are a few reasons why you might see a fee when withdrawing cash, depending on where and how the transaction is processed.
> Fees for getting cash can depend on the ATM you use and the account option selected during the transaction.
> Horizon Bank and SCB ATMs: There are absolutely no charges for using Horizon Bank or SCB ATMs.
> Third-Party and Overseas ATMs: The bank that operates a third-party or foreign ATM may levy its own surcharge; Horizon does not control that fee.
> Credit Account and Cash Advances: If you have both a Horizon Link credit-card account and a savings account, selecting the credit account option at an ATM will process the withdrawal as a cash advance.
